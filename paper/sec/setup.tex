\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Notation.} A prompt has three sections: the system message (S), the
image (I), which expands to many visual tokens, and the task or question (T). An
ordering is written as its sequence, so \sit{} is system-image-question and
\sti{} is system-question-image. An ordering may repeat a section: \stit{}
repeats the question after the image, \sitit{} repeats the image after the
question, and \sititrev{} is \sitit{} with the second image copy spatially
reversed. The two baselines are \sit{}, which is what most inference code emits
by default, and \sti{}, which is what a reader is told to do when looking for
something specific.

\paragraph{Models.} Qwen3-VL-8B is the primary model and carries every analysis.
For breadth we also run Gemma-3-27B, Gemma-4-31B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B
and LLaVA-1.5, with the Gemma models loaded in 4-bit on a single GPU. All results
use greedy decoding with a fixed answer-token budget, and every ordering of a
given benchmark shares one system message, one question string per item, one
image preprocessing path and one scoring function, so the ordering is the only
difference between columns.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.} Three benchmarks carry the diagnosis recapped in
Section~\ref{sec:background}: NaturalBench \citep{naturalbench2024},
$1{,}900$ groups ($7{,}600$ pairs); POPE \citep{pope2023}, $9{,}000$ items over
its random, popular and adversarial splits; and Winoground
\citep{winoground2022}, $400$ examples ($1{,}600$ items). The generality study
of Section~\ref{sec:generality} adds fourteen benchmarks not tested in prior
work: RF20-VL, used in two forms, as an object-detection benchmark scored by mAP
over 20 datasets and $3{,}970$ images with multi-class prompting, and as a
$24{,}449$-question yes/no presence task over the same images in 7 categories;
nine image benchmarks (BLINK \citep{blink2024},
CV-Bench \citep{cambrian2024}, HR-Bench, MMStar \citep{mmstar2024}, MMVP,
RealWorldQA, WorldMedQA-V, TallyQA \citep{tallyqa2019}, VQAv2 \citep{vqav2});
and four video QA benchmarks (MVBench \citep{mvbench2024}, NExT-QA
\citep{nextqa2021}, MSVD-QA \citep{msvdqa2017}, TGIF-QA \citep{tgifqa2017}). For
NaturalBench and Winoground we pair on the group or example index and report an
exact two-sided McNemar test alongside a paired bootstrap 95\% confidence
interval over $5{,}000$ resamples.

\paragraph{Interpretability tools.} All mechanistic analyses use a logit lens
\citep{logitlens2020, tunedlens2023}, projecting intermediate residual-stream
states through the unembedding to read out per-layer top tokens, either at the
answer position or over image patches. The attention knockout follows the
edge-severing methodology used for factual recall in language models
\citep{attnknockout2023, rome2022}.
