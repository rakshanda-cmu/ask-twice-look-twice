\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Notation.} A prompt has three sections: the system message (S), the
image (I), which expands to many visual tokens, and the task or question (T). We
write an ordering as the sequence of its sections, so \sit{} is
system-image-question and \sti{} is system-question-image. An ordering may
repeat a section: \stit{} repeats the question after the image, \sitit{} repeats
the image after the question, and \sititrev{} is \sitit{} with the second image
copy spatially reversed. The two baselines are \sit{}, which is what most
inference code emits by default, and \sti{}, which is what a reader is told to
do when looking for something specific.

\paragraph{Models.} Qwen3-VL-8B is the primary model and carries every analysis.
For breadth we also run Gemma-3-27B, Gemma-4-31B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B
and LLaVA-1.5. Gemma-3-27B and Gemma-4-31B are loaded in 4-bit on a single GPU.
All results use greedy decoding with a fixed answer-token budget, and every
ordering of a given benchmark shares one system message, one question string per
item, one image preprocessing path and one scoring function, so that the
ordering is the only difference between columns.

\paragraph{Benchmarks.} The four benchmarks that carry both the accuracy ladder
and the mechanistic analysis are NaturalBench \citep{naturalbench2024} with
$1{,}900$ groups ($7{,}600$ image-question pairs, scored by pair, question,
image and group accuracy), POPE \citep{pope2023} with $9{,}000$ items across its
random, popular and adversarial splits, Winoground \citep{winoground2022} with
$400$ examples ($1{,}600$ items, scored by group accuracy), and RF20, a
$24{,}449$-item presence-detection benchmark built from 20 detection datasets.
For the breadth study of Section~\ref{sec:results} we add nine image benchmarks
(BLINK \citep{blink2024}, CV-Bench \citep{cambrian2024}, HR-Bench, MMStar
\citep{mmstar2024}, MMVP, RealWorldQA, WorldMedQA-V, TallyQA
\citep{tallyqa2019} and VQAv2 \citep{vqav2}) and four video QA benchmarks
(MVBench \citep{mvbench2024}, NExT-QA \citep{nextqa2021}, MSVD-QA
\citep{msvdqa2017} and TGIF-QA \citep{tgifqa2017}).

\paragraph{Significance.} For NaturalBench and Winoground we pair on the group
or example index and report both an exact two-sided McNemar test and a paired
bootstrap 95\% confidence interval over $5{,}000$ resamples.

\paragraph{Interpretability tools.} All mechanistic analyses use a logit lens
\citep{logitlens2020, tunedlens2023}, projecting intermediate residual-stream
states through the unembedding to read out per-layer top tokens, either at the
answer position or over image patches. The attention knockout follows the
edge-severing methodology used for factual recall in language models
\citep{attnknockout2023, rome2022}.
