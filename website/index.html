Ask Twice, Look Twice

Interactive supplement — watching the question-first paradox and its fix, layer by layer.

A vision-language model reads system, image, and question tokens as one stream and answers from the final position. Where the question goes changes the answer. Placing it first (STI), before the long span of image tokens, makes the model commit early to an image-anchored, often wrong answer — even though it perceives the scene correctly. Re-presenting the (image, question) after the image (SITIT) restores the decoder's access to the question and fixes it, with no training.

Below are four clear NaturalBench yes/no cases where STI answers wrong and SITIT answers right. Each pair of animations is a per-layer logit lens: scrub through depth and watch the wrong answer lock in under STI while the correct answer emerges under SITIT. Images are shown at low resolution (few image tokens) and zoomed.

  • SIT System · Image · Task question-last (baseline)

  • SITIT System · Image · Task · Image · Task image echo (ours, best)
